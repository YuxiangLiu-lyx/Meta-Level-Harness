# Course Grade Entry

## Natural-Language Scenario

The system manages course modules and gradebook entries in a learning-management system.

The people involved are instructors of record, teaching assistants, learners, and coordinators. One person may hold both the instructor of record and teaching assistant roles at the same time.

The resources are modules and grade entries. Every module records its course, instructor of record, roster, prerequisite level, release time, locked setting, and status.

Every grade entry records its learner, module, who posted it, and its status.

Each requester carries their completed level.

Each request carries the current time, whether it is on the institution network, and the preview window.

An instructor of record or teaching assistant may enter a grade only while the grade entry is in draft status.

An instructor of record or teaching assistant must not enter a grade when the grade entry is not in draft status.

A person who is neither an instructor of record nor a teaching assistant must not enter a grade.

When the grade entry is in draft status, an instructor of record or teaching assistant may enter the grade if no other rule blocks the request.

A request by an instructor of record to enter a draft grade entry that trips no prohibition above must be able to succeed.
